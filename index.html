🔧 Customize ChatGPT for Your Needs

Set up Custom Instructions
  • What should ChatGPT know about you?
    Mention your profession (e.g., designer, developer, student, business owner), your goals (e.g., productivity, content creation, research), and tools you use often (e.g., Notion, Canva, ChatGPT, Figma).
  • How should ChatGPT respond?
    Choose your style: professional, casual, detailed, quick responses, humorous tone, etc. Add if you prefer summaries, bullet points, or step-by-step guidance.
